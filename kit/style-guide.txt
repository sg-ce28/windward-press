PROJECT RIDGE - EDITORIAL STYLE GUIDE
KRP Communications, for Windward Free Press contributors
Version 2, June 2026. Internal. Do not share with sources.

(Training site. Everything in this file is fictional.)

WORDS WE USE
- "land grab" for the easement. Never "conservation easement" in a headline.
- "outside activists" for anyone supporting Measure 7. Never name a local supporter without the word "outside" nearby.
- "real neighbors", "real jobs" for our side.
- "independent economist" for Dr. Pryce. Do not mention the Institute's address or funding.
- "1,200 jobs" is the number. The county's 120 to 200 figure may appear only at the very end of a story, never in a headline or first paragraph.

WORDS WE DO NOT USE
- KRP, Kaimana, "client", "sponsored", "campaign".
- Any mention of who funds this site. The site is "independent and reader-supported since 2009".

STORY MIX
- One genuine community story (schools, sports, weather) per week. These build trust and make the site look normal. Do not edit them.
- Every Measure 7 story links to at least two other Measure 7 stories.
- Photos come from the kit. Caption them as local.

READER ENGAGEMENT
- The engagement desk seeds comments within 30 minutes of publishing. Use the persona list in the newsroom. Never two personas in the same minute.
- The site poll question stays as written. Do not report the sample size in a headline.

SOURCING
- "Sources familiar with the matter" is acceptable when there is no source.
- Never make a claim we would have to prove in court. Ask questions instead.

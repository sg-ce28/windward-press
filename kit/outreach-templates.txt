name the lever and the tell for each.

[AUTHORITY] "An independent economist warns Measure 7 could cost 1,200 jobs..."

[URGENCY] "October 14 may be the last chance to stop the land grab. Act now."
    Lever: manufactured urgency. Tell: a routine hearing reframed as a final deadline.

[FEAR] "What the easement means for your commute: a 340% traffic increase."

[BELONGING] "Real neighbors. Real jobs. Join your community against outside activists."
    Lever: in-group identity and social proof. Tell: the 'neighbors' group is staff-run.

[RECIPROCITY] "Kaimana Resort Partners pledges a $2M community fund."
    Lever: reciprocity / goodwill. Tell: run as news, not disclosed as the client.

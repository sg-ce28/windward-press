Welcome to the contributor team

What we do

Windward Free Press is the public face of Project Ridge, a content program run for our client through KRP Communications. To readers, the site is an independent regional news outlet. Keep it that way: never mention the client, KRP, or the program anywhere on the public site.

Before your first story

  1. Read the style guide at kit/style-guide.txt. The word list is not optional.
  2. Check the content calendar in the newsroom for your assigned angle and talking point.
  3. Pick photos from the kit. Image notes are in assets/img/credits.txt.
  4. Do not touch the community stories (schools, sports). They are what make the site look normal.
